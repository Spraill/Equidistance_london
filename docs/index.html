    How Halfway House works

    Add where everyone is starting from and the kind of place you fancy. Halfway House works out how long each person would take to reach every pub, park, museum or gallery across London, then suggests the places where nobody gets a raw deal.

    Two kinds of halfway

    As the crow flies is the centre of the smallest circle around everyone: the same straight-line distance for whoever is furthest out. It often lands somewhere awkward to reach.

    By transport is the spot where the longest journey is as short as it can be. The shading on the map shows how close each area comes to that.

    How journeys are worked out

    For instant results, it models the tube, Elizabeth line, Overground, DLR, trams and main rail routes station by station, with time to walk to the platform, wait and change lines, plus buses and walking at either end. Then it asks the TfL Journey Planner for the real routes and times for the best suggestions. Results marked Live come from TfL; Estimate means TfL couldn't be reached.
